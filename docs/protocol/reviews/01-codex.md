# Revue Codex n°1 de la spécification du protocole (2026-11-01) — verdict : revise

- **Critique — [spec §9.4, §12, §20.2](../spec.md):** admission omits ADR 0030’s structural guard; ChangeSig/history remain reserved despite ADR 0031’s activation prerequisites. Specify the complete receive chain and mandatory capabilities; `core/2` alone cannot establish the accepted Byzantine guarantees.
- **Critique — §7.3, §10.5:** RESET can overtake queued old REPO messages; after its echo, those untagged messages can enter the new generation. Require atomic queue cancellation, generation checks on asynchronous deliveries, an explicit stream barrier, and defined u32 exhaustion behavior.
- **Critique — §6.2, §16.1:** one writer can occupy all 65,536 attestation keys; several peers can retain the entire offered-object budget through periodic WANTs. Shared ceilings lack reserved shares, contrary to ADR 0031. Bound ownership and guarantee honest-peer admission.
- **Mineur — §7.3, §10.2, §16.1 — hypothesis:** a full REPO queue stops the same stream carrying RESET/TRUST; reset-dependent recovery could consequently wait on itself. Specify and model progress under full queues; transport credit alone supplies no control bypass ([RFC 9000 §4.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9000.html#section-4.1)).
- **Mineur — §5.6, A.1:** an oversized Message consumes the bridge’s exceptional single-message allowance until fully pulled, blocking subsequent typing and presence to that peer. Reserve separate capacity for inline traffic or narrow the advertised responsiveness guarantee.
- **Mineur — §7.2, §8.1, §13:** bulk lacks precise outstanding-request matching, final partial-group semantics, Bao-total/advertised-size checks before allocation, and consistent malformed-message scope. Define these, unsolicited-response handling, and BUSY retry accounting.
- **Mineur — §3.2; ADR 0034 migration step 2:** range checks and small-order blacklisting do not establish point decodability: `02 00…00` is an off-curve counterexample. Require actual point decoding at every entry point, including reload/import, with negative vectors ([RFC 8032 §5.1.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8032.html#section-5.1.3)).
- **Mineur — §9.2:** banning indefinite lengths, tags and duplicates does not define deterministic CBOR: preferred widths, float representation and map ordering remain unspecified. Select a profile and its decoder acceptance policy ([RFC 8949 §4.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8949.html#section-4.2)).
- **Mineur — §9.2–§9.5; `cddl/repo.cddl`:** prose requires `targetId` universally, but handshake CDDL omits it; anchor/head limits and laser-coordinate coupling remain comments. Reconcile schemas and prose, including nested unknown-field handling and decoding-depth bounds.
- **Mineur — §21:** no public REPO/PRESENCE CBOR vectors exist; `bridge-message` contains only `{type:"sync"}`, valid as opaque bridge payload but insufficient for repository CDDL. Add positive/negative schema vectors, negotiation context, reset traces and bulk-tail cases.
- **Mineur — §5.5, §6.2:** ATTEST loss is permitted and described as repaired within 15 seconds, but periodic retransmission is specified only for TRUST. A final lost attestation need not return before reconnection. Require periodic reconciliation or reliable pending delivery.
- **Mineur — §6.1:** an `ops` response cannot contain the whole 1024-op journal within 81,920 bytes; batching, causal ordering and continuation are undefined. Specify bounded batches and eventual completion, including after sealing.
- **Critique — §19; ADR 0034 migration:** step 8 activates before step 10 verifies RESET, and lacks ADR 0031’s explicit activation gates. Move model verification and safety prerequisites before cutover; stage proposed-vector enforcement so intermediate merges pass; record the decided direct switch and unconditional spill/eviction removal.
- **Checks passed:** sampled accepted frame lengths and genesis/Add/policy SHA-256 values match; signature domains and signed-byte layouts are explicit (§3.3–§4), and authenticated HELLO prevents intermediary capability stripping (§17). Signature verification was not executed.
revise
