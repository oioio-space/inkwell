M2 methodology:
  Before and after: go test ./internal/app -run '^$' -bench '^BenchmarkRelay$' -count=10 -benchmem
  Comparison: benchstat old.txt new.txt
  Run via mise exec -- scripts/heavy.sh, with GOTMPDIR and GOCACHE under ~/.cache/inkwell.
  BenchmarkRelay/Message feeds a real mux repo queue, calls Wiring.step
  (including trust/attestation polls), and checks the bridge envelope.
  BenchmarkRelay/Idle measures the empty-queue step; no network or trust
  initialization is timed. The message's remaining allocation is the mux queue.
  Reuse one already-canceled polling context per Wiring. Keep the live wiring
  context, goroutine ownership, and transport-close cancellation unchanged.
  Both timings improve significantly (p<0.05); bytes and allocations decrease.
